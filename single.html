The Journey

Four years of
becoming.

From nervous freshers to the people who shaped this place — here is the story of Batch '26, told in the moments that mattered most.

2022 — Year I
The Beginning

Strangers walked in through those gates, wide-eyed and uncertain. They'd leave as something far more — as family. The first semester was chaos, caffeine, and the slow discovery that everyone was figuring it out together.

2023 — Year II
Finding Ground

The second year brought confidence and competition in equal measure. Late nights in the common room, friendships forged over shared deadlines, the first big wins — and the losses that taught us more.

2024 — Year III
The Peak

This was the year everything came together. Leadership roles, passion projects, the festival everyone still talks about. Batch '26 left their mark on every wall of this institution — and on everyone in it.

2025–26 — Year IV
The Final Chapter

The last year was bittersweet before it even began. They savoured every ordinary day knowing it was extraordinary. And now they walk out — not as students, but as people ready to change the world.
